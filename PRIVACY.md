# Privacy Policy

**Alert Train Live** is built to help you follow your train journey - without tracking you.

## We do not track you

We do not collect, sell, or share your personal information. We do not run analytics. We do not profile you. We do not use advertising identifiers.

## Your data stays on your phone

Train trackings, alarms, and preferences you set in the app are stored only on your device. We do not operate accounts, cloud sync, or a backend that stores your journey data.

## No ads, no subscriptions, no purchases

Alert Train Live is free forever. There are:

- No ads
- No analytics or tracking SDKs
- No subscriptions
- No in-app purchases
- No paid upgrades

## What the app does

You can look up trains, save the ones you care about, see live status and schedule information for your journey, set arrival-style alarms, and view coach layout details when available. To show schedules and live status, the app fetches public train timetables and running status from the internet. Only the train number and date you ask about are used for this. Optional Android permissions (notifications, battery, overlay, and similar) are used only so those features can work on your device - never to track you.

## App updates

The app may check a public release page for a newer version and, if you choose, download that update to your device. That check is only for app updates - not for tracking.
